## What's new

**Sortable tables** — Recurring, Rules, and Portfolio pages now have clickable column headers to sort ascending, descending, or reset. Sort state is preserved in the URL.

**Post-import celebration** — After importing transactions, a toast celebrates your progress and suggests your next step.

**Collapsible sidebar** — The sidebar is now organized into collapsible sections. Your collapse state is saved per-device.

**AI unified inbox** — All AI suggestions, review queue items, CFO briefings, and chat proposals are now consolidated in one filterable inbox at `/ai/unified-inbox`.
